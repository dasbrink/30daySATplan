Day 26 — Fix Your Foundational Gaps

Think of SAT Math like Lego blocks. Every concept you learn sits on top of earlier ones. If a block near the bottom is missing or wobbly, everything above it becomes unstable — no matter how hard you study the advanced stuff.

Today is about finding and fixing those missing blocks. This is the most valuable work you can do. One hour here is worth more than five hours drilling practice questions on shaky foundations.

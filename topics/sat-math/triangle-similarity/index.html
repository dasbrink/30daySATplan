Similar Triangles on the SAT

SAT Math · Geometry and Trigonometry · Updated October 2026

Similar triangle questions look like geometry, but almost all of the work is a proportion. Where students go wrong is not the arithmetic. It is matching the wrong sides, usually because the two triangles are drawn turned or one sits inside the other.

What I tell my students first

Two triangles are similar when their angles match. Once you know that, every pair of matching sides has the same ratio, called the scale factor.

Before you write a proportion, name the matching vertices in order, for example $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$. Then $AB$ matches $DE$, $BC$ matches $EF$ and $AC$ matches $DF$. Lengths scale by the scale factor $k$, and areas scale by $k^2$.

How to know the triangles are similar

You only need one of these. On the SAT the first one does most of the work, because the question usually hands you a pair of equal angles without saying so.

A segment drawn parallel to one side of a triangle always cuts off a smaller triangle similar to the big one. The shared top angle is one pair, and the parallel line makes the base angles equal. That picture is the most common similarity question on the test.

Three examples, step by step

The nested triangle

In the figure below, $DE$ is parallel to $BC$. $AD = 4$, $DB = 6$ and $DE = 5$. What is $BC$?

  1. Triangle $ADE$ is similar to triangle $ABC$, because they share angle $A$ and the parallel lines make the base angles equal.
  2. Match the whole side, not the piece. $AD$ matches $AB$, and $AB = 4 + 6 = 10$.
  3. $\dfrac{DE}{BC} = \dfrac{AD}{AB}$, so $\dfrac{5}{BC} = \dfrac{4}{10}$ and $BC = 12.5$.
  4. The slip I see most is using $DB = 6$ in place of $AB$. That gives $7.5$, which is one of the wrong choices every time.

$DE \parallel BC$, so $\triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC$. Compare $AD$ with all of $AB$, not with $DB$.

The shadow problem

A person $6$ feet tall casts a shadow $4$ feet long. At the same time, a tree casts a shadow $18$ feet long. How tall is the tree?

  1. The sun hits both at the same angle, and both stand straight up, so the two right triangles are similar.
  2. Keep heights on top: $\dfrac{6}{4} = \dfrac{h}{18}$.
  3. $h = \dfrac{6 \cdot 18}{4} = 27$ feet.

Area from a scale factor

Two similar triangles have areas $16$ and $49$. A side of the larger triangle is $14$. How long is the matching side of the smaller one?

  1. Areas scale by $k^2$, so $k^2 = \dfrac{49}{16}$ and $k = \dfrac{7}{4}$.
  2. The smaller side is $14 \div \dfrac{7}{4} = 8$.
  3. If you divide $14$ by $\dfrac{49}{16}$ you are treating the area ratio as a length ratio, and the answer comes out wrong.

Where students go wrong

  • Using the piece instead of the whole. In a nested triangle, compare the small side with the whole side of the big triangle. $AD$ goes with $AB$, never with $DB$.
  • Matching sides by where they sit on the page. Turned or flipped triangles put matching sides in different places. Match by the equal angles, then write the vertices in the same order.
  • Scaling area like a length. If the scale factor is $3$, the area is $9$ times as large, not $3$ times.
  • Mixing the order in the proportion. Keep small over large on both sides, or large over small on both sides.
  • Assuming similarity from looks. You need a reason: a shared angle and a parallel line, two right angles plus a shared angle, or sides in one ratio.

Practice questions

Name the matching vertices before you set up anything.

Easy
Triangle $ABC$ is similar to triangle $DEF$, with $A$, $B$ and $C$ corresponding to $D$, $E$ and $F$. If $AB = 6$, $DE = 9$ and $BC = 8$, what is $EF$?
  • A$11$
  • B$12$
  • C$\dfrac{16}{3}$
  • D$13.5$
Show solution
Answer: B. The scale factor from $ABC$ to $DEF$ is $\dfrac{9}{6} = 1.5$, so $EF = 8 \cdot 1.5 = 12$. Choice A adds $3$ instead of multiplying, and choice C uses the ratio upside down.

Answer: $90$. Areas scale by the square of the scale factor: $10 \cdot 3^2 = 90$.
Medium
Two similar triangles have perimeters $18$ and $30$. A side of the smaller triangle has length $6$. What is the length of the matching side of the larger triangle?
  • A$10$
  • B$12$
  • C$18$
  • D$3.6$
Show solution
Answer: A. Perimeters scale like lengths, so the scale factor is $\dfrac{30}{18} = \dfrac{5}{3}$. The side is $6 \cdot \dfrac{5}{3} = 10$. Choice B adds the $6$ again, and choice D uses the factor upside down.

Answer: $16$. $\dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{AE}{AC}$, so $\dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{4}{AC}$ and $AC = 16$.
Hard
Two similar triangles have areas $16$ and $49$. A side of the larger triangle has length $14$. What is the length of the matching side of the smaller triangle?
  • A$8$
  • B$\dfrac{32}{7}$
  • C$12$
  • D$24.5$
Show solution
Answer: A. $k^2 = \dfrac{49}{16}$, so $k = \dfrac{7}{4}$ and the side is $14 \cdot \dfrac{4}{7} = 8$. Choice B scales the side by the area ratio.
HardStudent-produced response
A right triangle has legs of length $6$ and $8$. A segment is drawn from the right angle perpendicular to the hypotenuse. What is the length of this segment?
Enter your answer.
Show solution
Answer: $4.8$ (or $\dfrac{24}{5}$). The hypotenuse is $10$. The segment splits the triangle into two smaller triangles similar to the original. Matching the original triangle with the small one that shares the leg of $6$: $\dfrac{h}{6} = \dfrac{8}{10}$, so $h = 4.8$. You can check with area: $\dfrac{1}{2}(6)(8) = \dfrac{1}{2}(10)h$.
Hard
Triangle $PQR$ has sides $PQ = 9$, $QR = 12$ and $PR = 15$. Triangle $STU$ is similar to triangle $PQR$, and its shortest side has length $6$. What is the perimeter of triangle $STU$?
  • A$18$
  • B$24$
  • C$30$
  • D$36$
Show solution
Answer: B. The scale factor is $\dfrac{6}{9} = \dfrac{2}{3}$. The perimeter of $PQR$ is $36$, so the perimeter of $STU$ is $36 \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} = 24$. Choice D is the perimeter of the original triangle.

In triangle $ABC$, $\overline{DE}$ is parallel to $\overline{BC}$, with $D$ on $\overline{AB}$ and $E$ on $\overline{AC}$. The area of triangle $ADE$ is $9$ and the area of the trapezoid $DBCE$ is $16$. If $AD = 6$, what is $AB$?
Enter your answer.
Show solution
Answer: $10$. The whole triangle has area $9 + 16 = 25$. The area ratio is $\dfrac{9}{25}$, so the side ratio is $\dfrac{3}{5}$. $AB = 6 \cdot \dfrac{5}{3} = 10$. Using $\dfrac{9}{16}$ compares the small triangle with the trapezoid, which is not a similar shape.

Scatterplots on the SAT

SAT Math · Problem-Solving and Data Analysis · Updated October 2026

Scatterplot questions are some of the friendliest points on the test, and students still give them away by reading the graph too quickly. Read the axis labels and the scale before you look at a single point. Half the wrong answers I see come from a student who assumed each grid line was $1$.

Where I would start

A scatterplot shows pairs of numbers as points. If the points rise from left to right, the association is positive. If they fall, it is negative. The tighter they hug a line, the stronger the association.

A line of best fit turns the cloud into an equation you can use. To predict, put the $x$-value into the equation. A residual is the actual value minus the predicted value.

A positive association. The gold line is a line of best fit: about half the points sit above it and half below.

What you will see

  • Describe the association. Positive or negative, strong or weak, linear or curved.
  • Predict a value. Use the line of best fit, not a single data point.
  • Find a residual. Actual minus predicted. Positive means the point is above the line.
  • Interpret the slope. The predicted change in $y$ for each increase of $1$ in $x$, in the units of the problem.
  • Interpret the $y$-intercept. The predicted $y$ when $x = 0$. Sometimes that makes sense in the story, and sometimes it does not.
  • Linear or exponential. If the points curve upward faster and faster, an exponential model fits better than a line.

Three examples

Predicting with the line

The line of best fit for a set of data is $y = 2.5x + 10$. What value of $y$ does it predict when $x = 8$?

  1. Substitute: $2.5(8) + 10$.
  2. $20 + 10 = 30$.
  3. That is a prediction from the line. An actual data point at $x = 8$ could be above or below it.

A residual

A data point is $(10, 52)$. The line of best fit is $y = 3x + 18$. What is the residual for this point?

  1. Predicted: $3(10) + 18 = 48$.
  2. Residual is actual minus predicted: $52 - 48 = 4$.
  3. The point sits $4$ units above the line.

Building the line from two points

A line of best fit passes through $(0, 40)$ and $(20, 10)$. What does it predict when $x = 12$?

  1. Slope: $\dfrac{10 - 40}{20 - 0} = -1.5$.
  2. The $y$-intercept is $40$, so $y = -1.5x + 40$.
  3. At $x = 12$: $-18 + 40 = 22$.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Not reading the scale. If each grid line on the $y$-axis is $5$, two lines up is $10$, not $2$.
  • Predicting from a point instead of the line. The question says "according to the line of best fit." Use the equation.
  • Residual the wrong way round. It is actual minus predicted. A point below the line has a negative residual.
  • Reading cause into a correlation. A strong association does not show that one thing causes the other.

Practice questions

For each one, decide first whether you need the line or a data point.

Easy
The line of best fit for a scatterplot is $y = 2.5x + 10$. What value of $y$ does the line predict for $x = 8$?
  • A$18$
  • B$20$
  • C$28$
  • D$30$
Show solution
Answer: D. $2.5(8) + 10 = 30$. Choice B forgets the $10$.

Answer: B. The line predicts $3(10) + 18 = 48$. The residual is $52 - 48 = 4$. Choice A subtracts in the wrong order.

Medium
A line of best fit passes through $(0, 40)$ and $(20, 10)$. What value of $y$ does the line predict for $x = 12$?
  • A$18$
  • B$22$
  • C$28$
  • D$52$
Show solution
Answer: B. The slope is $-1.5$ and the intercept is $40$, so $y = -1.5(12) + 40 = 22$. Choice C adds instead of subtracting.
MediumStudent-produced response

Answer: $50$. $-0.4x + 50 = 30$, so $-0.4x = -20$ and $x = 50$.
Hard
Data are modeled by the exponential function $y = 200(1.05)^x$. What value of $y$ does the model predict for $x = 2$?
  • A$205$
  • B$210$
  • C$220$
  • D$220.5$
Show solution
Answer: D. $200(1.05)^2 = 200(1.1025) = 220.5$. Choice C adds $5\%$ twice to $200$ instead of compounding.
HardStudent-produced response
A line of best fit passes through the points $(3, 14)$ and $(9, 32)$. What value of $y$ does the line predict for $x = 15$?
Enter your answer.
Show solution
Answer: $50$. The slope is $\dfrac{32 - 14}{9 - 3} = 3$, so $y = 3x + 5$. At $x = 15$: $45 + 5 = 50$.
Hard
The line of best fit for three data points is $y = 2x + 1$. The points are $(1, 4)$, $(2, 4)$ and $(3, 8)$. What is the sum of the three residuals?
  • A$-1$
  • B$0$
  • C$1$
  • D$3$
Show solution
Answer: C. The predictions are $3$, $5$ and $7$. The residuals are $4 - 3 = 1$, $4 - 5 = -1$ and $8 - 7 = 1$, which add to $1$.
HardStudent-produced response

Enter your answer.
Show solution
Answer: $5$. $y$ is in thousands, so $y = 13$. $-2.2x + 24 = 13$, so $x = 5$. Using $13{,}000$ for $y$ is the trap.

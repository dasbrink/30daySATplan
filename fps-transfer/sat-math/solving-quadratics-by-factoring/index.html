Solving Quadratic Equations by Factoring

SAT Math · Advanced Math · Updated October 2026

The one idea to hold onto

If two numbers multiply to $0$, at least one of them is $0$. That is the whole method. Get the equation equal to $0$, factor the other side, then set each factor equal to $0$.

The equation must equal $0$ before you factor. From $x(x - 4) = 21$ you cannot conclude $x = 21$; you first rewrite it as $x^2 - 4x - 21 = 0$.

Factoring is usually the fastest way to solve a quadratic on the SAT. The points are lost by factoring before the equation equals $0$, by dividing both sides by $x$ and losing a solution, and by sign slips when reading the solutions off the factors.

Reading the concept isn't enough. The score comes from practicing in the real digital SAT format and finding the exact mistakes you keep making.

How to solve one, step by step

Example 1: moving everything to one side

Solve $x(x - 4) = 21$.

  1. Expand and move $21$ over: $x^2 - 4x - 21 = 0$.
  2. Factor: two numbers that multiply to $-21$ and add to $-4$ are $-7$ and $3$. So $(x - 7)(x + 3) = 0$.
  3. $x = 7$ or $x = -3$.

Example 2: a leading coefficient that is not $1$

Solve $2x^2 - 5x - 3 = 0$.

  1. Multiply $a \cdot c = 2 \cdot (-3) = -6$. Two numbers that multiply to $-6$ and add to $-5$ are $-6$ and $1$.
  2. Split the middle term: $2x^2 - 6x + x - 3 = 2x(x - 3) + 1(x - 3) = (2x + 1)(x - 3)$.
  3. $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ or $x = 3$.

Example 3: a word problem

A rectangle is $3$ units longer than it is wide, and its area is $40$. Find the width.

  1. $w(w + 3) = 40$, so $w^2 + 3w - 40 = 0$.
  2. Factor: $(w + 8)(w - 5) = 0$, so $w = -8$ or $w = 5$.
  3. A width cannot be negative, so the width is $5$.

The mistakes that cost points

  • Factoring before the equation equals $0$. The zero product rule only works with $0$ on one side.
  • Dividing by $x$. In $x^2 = 9x$, dividing by $x$ loses the solution $x = 0$.
  • Keeping the signs from the factors. $(x + 5) = 0$ gives $x = -5$, not $5$.
  • Missing a common factor. In $3x^2 - 12 = 0$, take out the $3$ first: $3(x^2 - 4) = 0$.
  • Keeping a negative length. In area and distance problems, throw away the negative solution.

Want to know exactly how it works?

The problem with most SAT prep

More practice tests will not close the gaps.

Generic prep wastes time on what your child already knows. The gaps that actually cost them points go unaddressed.

SAT Math is cumulative. A gap in a foundational concept does not just affect one question — it affects every question built on top of it. Finding and fixing that one gap can unlock an entire category of problems.

SAT Math knowledge builds like Lego blocks. If a foundational piece is missing, everything above it becomes unstable — no matter how much time a student spends on higher topics. This program finds the missing pieces first. Then it fixes them specifically, not generally.

What the research says

Anders Ericsson's landmark research at Florida State University established that improvement does not come from more repetition — it comes from identifying specific weaknesses and targeting them directly. He called this deliberate practice. Taking another full practice test without fixing identified gaps is not deliberate practice. It is repetition without progress. This program is built entirely on that principle.

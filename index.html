The problem with most SAT prep

Generic prep wastes time on what your child already knows.

Most SAT courses cover every topic for every student — regardless of what they individually need. A student who already understands algebra spends weeks on it anyway. The gaps that actually cost them points go unaddressed.

SAT Math is also cumulative. A gap in a foundational concept doesn't just affect one question — it affects every question built on top of it. Finding and fixing that one gap can unlock an entire category of problems.

This program starts with a full diagnostic before any studying begins. Your child's path through the 30 days is shaped entirely by their own results.
